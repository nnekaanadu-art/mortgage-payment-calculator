# Mortgage Lab: Mortgage Payment Calculator

**An interactive Canadian and US mortgage planning prototype that helps users explore payment options, understand long-term borrowing costs, and compare financing scenarios.**

**Product Category:** Digital Product Experience | Financial Services

**[Launch Interactive Prototype](https://nnekaanadu-art.github.io/mortgage-payment-calculator/)**

## Overview

Mortgage planning involves more than calculating a monthly payment. Buyers need to understand how interest rates, amortization periods, payment frequencies, mortgage insurance, and upfront costs affect their overall financial commitments.

I developed Mortgage Lab to explore how an interactive digital experience can simplify complex financial calculations, make assumptions transparent, and support more informed mortgage planning.

The prototype demonstrates my approach to customer-facing product design, financial services technology, user experience, and AI-assisted prototyping.

## The Problem

Mortgage planning can be challenging because:

- Canadian and US mortgage structures, terminology, and calculation conventions differ.
- Buyers may focus on monthly payments without understanding total borrowing costs.
- Payment frequency and amortization choices can affect repayment schedules and interest costs.
- Mortgage insurance and upfront expenses introduce additional financial considerations.
- First-time homebuyer incentives can vary by jurisdiction and eligibility.
- Comparing alternative mortgage scenarios often requires repeating calculations across multiple tools.

## The Solution

Mortgage Lab brings mortgage calculations, financing assumptions, repayment analysis, and optional scenario comparison into one interactive experience.

### Key Capabilities

**Canadian and US Mortgage Calculations**

Estimate mortgage payments using country-specific calculation assumptions.

Users can explore how financing inputs affect estimated payments and overall borrowing costs.

**Flexible Payment Options**

Review estimated monthly, accelerated biweekly, and weekly mortgage payments.

Explore how payment schedules influence repayment patterns and interest costs.

**Mortgage Cost Summary**

View a consolidated summary of:

- Mortgage principal
- Mortgage insurance
- Total interest
- Total mortgage payments
- Monthly, biweekly, and weekly payment estimates
- Estimated cash required at closing

**Amortization Analysis**

Explore how mortgage payments are allocated between principal and interest over the amortization period.

Review estimated repayment schedules and long-term borrowing costs.

**Mortgage Balance Visualization**

The amortization chart updates as you change the mortgage inputs and shows both balance curves when comparison mode is on.

**Mortgage Insurance Estimates**

Explore illustrative mortgage insurance assumptions and their potential impact on financing costs.

**Optional Scenario Comparison**

Start with a single mortgage scenario to keep the experience focused.

Activate a second scenario only when comparison is needed, allowing users to evaluate differences in payments and borrowing costs.

**First-Time Homebuyer Considerations**

Explore illustrative first-time buyer incentives and location-based assumptions, including selected Canadian jurisdictions.

These estimates are intended for demonstration and are not maintained as current tax or incentive guidance.

## Product Approach

Mortgage Lab reflects several product design principles:

1. **User-centred simplicity:** Present complex mortgage calculations in a clear, accessible experience.
2. **Progressive disclosure:** Keep the default experience focused on one mortgage scenario and reveal comparison functionality only when requested.
3. **Transparent calculations:** Distinguish mortgage principal, insurance, interest, and total payments.
4. **Visual understanding:** Use amortization charts and repayment schedules to make long-term borrowing costs easier to understand.
5. **Context-aware experiences:** Account for differences between Canadian and US mortgage assumptions.
6. **Practical decision support:** Organize financial information so users can explore trade-offs before consulting a qualified professional.
7. **Iterative improvement:** Refine the interface and calculations through testing, feedback, and repeated validation.

## Technology and Development

Mortgage Lab is an independent portfolio prototype developed through AI-assisted prototyping and iterative product direction.

**Technology:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The application is designed to run directly in a web browser without installation or account creation.

The development approach emphasizes:

- Translating financial planning requirements into interactive user workflows.
- Designing a responsive, customer-facing experience.
- Structuring mortgage calculations and displaying results clearly.
- Supporting country-specific financing assumptions.
- Using progressive disclosure to manage interface complexity.
- Iteratively testing calculations, visualizations, and user interactions.

The prototype demonstrates product thinking, interactive financial experiences, and user-centred design rather than a production mortgage lending platform.

## Assumptions and Limitations

Mortgage Lab is an illustrative planning prototype.

Calculations are based on the inputs and assumptions implemented in the application.

Mortgage rates, taxes, insurance requirements, rebates, and government incentives may change over time.

The prototype does not automatically maintain or update these assumptions to reflect regulatory changes.

Actual mortgage terms, eligibility requirements, closing costs, and lender calculations may differ.
