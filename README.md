# Mortgage Payment Calculator

**An interactive Canadian and US mortgage planning prototype designed to help users explore mortgage payments, financing scenarios, and long-term borrowing costs.**

## Overview

Understanding mortgage affordability involves more than calculating a monthly payment. Buyers must consider interest rates, amortization periods, payment frequencies, mortgage insurance, and other factors that influence borrowing costs.

I developed this prototype to explore how an interactive digital experience can simplify mortgage calculations, make financing assumptions more transparent, and help users compare potential scenarios.

The project demonstrates my approach to product design, financial services technology, user experience, and AI-assisted prototyping.

## The Problem

Mortgage planning can be challenging because:

- Canadian and US mortgage structures and terminology differ.
- Payment frequency and amortization choices affect borrowing costs.
- Mortgage insurance requirements can add complexity.
- Buyers may struggle to understand the long-term impact of financing decisions.
- Comparing mortgage scenarios often requires multiple calculations.

## The Solution

An interactive mortgage planning tool that brings key calculations and scenario comparisons into one accessible experience.

### Key Capabilities

**Canadian and US Mortgage Calculations**

Explore mortgage payment estimates using country-specific assumptions and calculation approaches.

**Flexible Payment Options**

Evaluate different mortgage payment frequencies and understand how payment schedules affect estimated borrowing costs.

**Amortization Analysis**

Review mortgage amortization schedules to understand how principal and interest payments change over time.

**Mortgage Balance Visualization**

Explore how an estimated outstanding mortgage balance changes throughout the amortization period.

**Mortgage Insurance Estimates**

Illustrate how mortgage insurance assumptions may influence financing costs.

**Scenario Comparison**

Compare alternative mortgage scenarios to better understand potential differences in payments and borrowing costs.

**First-Time Buyer Considerations**

Explore illustrative information about location-based homebuyer incentives and related assumptions.

## Product Approach

The prototype reflects several product design principles:

1. **User-centred simplicity:** Present complex mortgage calculations through an accessible interface.
2. **Transparency:** Make financing inputs, assumptions, and calculated results understandable.
3. **Scenario-based exploration:** Allow users to compare alternatives rather than rely on a single estimate.
4. **Financial education:** Help users understand how mortgage variables influence estimated outcomes.
5. **Practical decision support:** Organize calculations and visualizations to support informed discussions and further research.

## Technology and Development

This is an independent portfolio prototype developed through AI-assisted prototyping and iterative product direction.

The application uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and is designed to run directly in a web browser.

The emphasis is on demonstrating product thinking, interactive workflow design, and financial calculation experiences rather than representing a production mortgage lending platform.

## Intended Audience

- Prospective homebuyers
- First-time homebuyers
- Individuals exploring Canadian or US mortgage scenarios
- Product and technology professionals interested in financial services applications

## Disclaimer

This mortgage calculator is an independent demonstration prototype created for educational and portfolio purposes.

Results should not be relied upon for financial decisions. Users should consult official sources and qualified professionals for current information.
